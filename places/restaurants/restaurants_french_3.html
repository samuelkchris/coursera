✨ Step into the Belle Époque ✨

Transport yourself to 1890s Paris in our stunning Art Nouveau dining room. Original Tiffany lamps, ornate mirrors, and vintage posters create the perfect ambiance for experiencing authentic brasserie cuisine. Our menu celebrates the golden age of French dining, when artists and intellectuals gathered in Parisian cafés to debate and dine.
