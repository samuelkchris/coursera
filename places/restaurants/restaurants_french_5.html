🌊 From the Shores of Brittany 🌊

Our crêpes and galettes are made the traditional Breton way, using buckwheat flour from Brittany and fresh ingredients sourced locally. Every crêpe is hand-made to order on our authentic cast-iron billig griddles. Pair your meal with French cider from Normandy for the complete experience!
